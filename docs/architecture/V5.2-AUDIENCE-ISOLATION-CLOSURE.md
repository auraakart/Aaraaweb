# Aaraagate V5.2 Audience RBAC and Tenant Isolation — Engineering Closure Evidence

## Decision boundary

This document records the scoped **non-production** engineering acceptance of the V5.2.x audience-RBAC and tenant-isolation slices. It does **not** certify production isolation, PostgreSQL row-level security (RLS), a payment processor, device-based access control, or partner-portal completeness. No migration or permission widening is implied.

The operational authority baseline is a live authenticated session bound to exactly the selected society plus current active society memberships (or scoped read-only auditor responsibility). Commercial PartnerMembership roles do not grant society operational roles. Independent-home privacy requests remain society-less and must never receive fabricated society authority.

## Negative authorization matrix

- A role for society B never authorizes requests scoped to society A; the selected society must have an active operational membership for the authenticated user, and commercial partner ownership alone is insufficient.
- Resident access is governed by current UnitOccupancy, including effective dates, not by historical ownership, inactive membership, or worker identity.
- A previously known worker, assignment, receipt or incident identifier does not bypass the explicit society predicates of that workflow.
- Household staff payment history belongs to the current resident: an idempotency-key replay after occupancy revocation is not an authorization bypass; no actual money movement is performed.
- Incident audit rows, privacy legal holds, retention restrictions and support evidence retain their original append-only or guarded mutation semantics.
- A gate check-in preflight is not the final authority. Within the serializable mutation, recheck the gate, assignment, worker, schedule and current occupants; use the transaction-verified recipient list for successful notifications. Existing idempotent replay of an already committed mutation remains supported.
- Guard-only actions continue to require the existing security-role and gate-assignment permissions; platform and partner administration do not inherit gate operational actions.

## Explicit remaining obligations — outside V5.2 engineering closure

1. PostgreSQL RLS policy design, activation and database-enforced isolation; the tenant GUC alone does not activate RLS.
2. Full operational productionization: infrastructure, real gateways, data-migration rehearsals, backups, live incidents, monitoring, external payment providers and physical hardware.
3. Separate partner-portal/commercial subscription functionality and subsequent V5.3+ branding/white-label deliverables.
4. Broader cross-service/worker background-job threat modelling, permission fuzzing and live penetration review before production rollout.
5. Staging/main promotion requires separate authorization; develop-only merging is not production deployment.

## Closure protocol

Do not mark V5.2 **COMPLETE** merely because the first unit tests pass. Verify the protected V5.2.29 CI, effective final merge SHA on `develop`, required security gates, and untouched `staging`/`main`. Reconcile the release manifest as a final evidence change after the successful merge. V5.0 and V5.1 are tracked separately and their manifest status is not silently rewritten by V5.2 closure.
