## Implemented boundaries

- V5.8.2 exports named CSV files through the platform save/share surface. Statement dates are India calendar dates, at most 366 inclusive days, with at most 1000 invoices and 1000 payments. Exceeding the bound is an explicit error, not silent truncation. This is maintenance activity with original record amounts, not a full ledger, opening/closing balance, net collection, utility/deposit/advance statement or fiscal invoice. Refunded records remain explicitly labelled. CSV cells neutralize spreadsheet formulas, quote delimiters and preserve Unicode. Demo sessions cannot request live financial statements.
- Current owner/tenant property authority is checked in a repeatable-read snapshot; tenants see their own payment evidence, while current verified owners retain the existing property-payment visibility. Feature guards, rejected foreign/stale relationships and current-context discard remain in force.
- V5.8.3 returns at most 51 rows for a 50-record page. Keyset ordering includes the UUID tie breaker and retains PostgreSQL microseconds rather than rounding cursor time to Dart milliseconds. Complaint title/number search is literal and parameterized; status/unit filters remain behind existing occupancy permissions. Payment summary aggregation is independent of visible history pages. Historical invoice activity is available through the bounded statement export; no separate full general-ledger UI is claimed.
- V5.8.4 adds scoped eight-language workflow/terms copy and standard Flutter localization delegates for platform controls. It does not claim that every historical product sentence is translated or native-speaker approved; the Assistant's deliberate English-only voice setting is unchanged.
- V5.8.5 observes helpdesk submissions, payment-order preparation and finance-export outcomes/duration/retries. Metrics are finite-dimension, in-memory telemetry exposed through the existing exporter boundary; hosted persistence/monitoring stays external. Client reports cannot prove a payment, consent, saved file, UAT or business outcome. Unconfirmed platform share outcomes remain unconfirmed.
- V5.8.6 derives the next onboarding action from current server readiness and saved source records. It does not create another mutable onboarding authority or mark navigation as completion. Session switches and failed refreshes clear prior guidance.
- V5.8.7 counts/presents the five existing provider declarations, makes missing fields explicit and keeps immutable booking snapshots and all checkout blockers intact. Business-approved policy values and live payment integration remain external; there is no invented refund/settlement promise.
- V5.8.8 extracts receipt rendering/export and history models into focused components, validates completed-payment receipt data, and regenerates the tenant inventory. No schema migration, production deployment, signing or store/device distribution is included.
